How a sale works

Four steps, usually done inside an hour for a standard load.

01

Call or enquire

Tell us the material and quantity — single lot or ongoing monthly supply. We'll quote the day's rate.

02

Pickup & weigh

We arrange transport for bulk lots, or you bring it in. Every load goes on the calibrated weighbridge.

03

Grade & confirm

Material is sorted by grade in front of you and the rate is confirmed before final billing.

04

Get paid

Cash or bank transfer, settled fast. A proper weighment slip and bill is given for every load.
